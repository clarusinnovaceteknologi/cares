## 1. Purpose and classification

This document reconciles dependencies and blocker classifications for **OD-001–OD-013**, preserved unresolved **PD-001–PD-012**, **ADR 0006 (Proposed)**, and **I-00–I-14** against the **authoritative Round 3.2.1 fidelity-corrected owner-approved principles and bounded conditions** (see [POSTGRESQL_ROUND3_2_OWNER_DECISION_REGISTER.md](./POSTGRESQL_ROUND3_2_OWNER_DECISION_REGISTER.md)).

**Classification scheme (required):**
- **Owner-approved principle (bounded):** authoritative governing principle and bounded conditions approved; not unconditional technical/implementation authorization.
- **Technically unresolved:** specific technical details, mechanisms, formulas, policies, predicates, validations, or approvals not explicitly finalized/approved.
- **Implementation-dependent:** requires implementation design/execution/validation evidence that is not present and not authorized in this round.
- **Governance-dependent:** requires separate formal governance action/approval (e.g. ADR status transition, increment authorization, policy approval) not provided here.

**Critical rule:** approval of a principle **does not** mark a technical blocker resolved. No item is closed/inferred as resolved unless explicitly evidenced and authorized within scope.
