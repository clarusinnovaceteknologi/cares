## 3. Mandatory interpretation notes

- **Technically prepared ≠ authorized:** conditional readiness or discussion of bounded structural preparation does not authorize implementation, execution, merge, deployment, or runtime exposure.
- **No fabricated authorization:** absence of an explicit owner authorization record means every increment remains unauthorized.
- **Separate authorization required per increment/scope:** use [POSTGRESQL_ROUND3_1_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_TEMPLATE.md](./POSTGRESQL_ROUND3_1_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_TEMPLATE.md); authorization for one increment does not authorize subsequent increments.
- **Tenant-runtime prohibition (current state):** I-12 remains blocked (PD-005/PD-006 and required validation). No tenant-safe runtime readiness is claimed.
- **Unresolved semantics:** I-06 final semantics remain blocked by OD-001/003/004/008 and ADR 0006 (Proposed); no finalized licensing/quota/accounting enforcement may be implemented or claimed.
