## 3. Technical audit summary (areas A–E)
Detailed evidence, classifications, and cross-document/OD reassessment are recorded in `docs/POSTGRESQL_LOGICAL_MODEL_ROUND1_1_FINAL_AUDIT.md`.

- **A. Source-device binding and identity ownership:** **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS (Proposed).** `asset_data_source` consistently recommended as authoritative stable binding owning immutable `source_device_key_id`; aliases resolve to same stable binding identity; optional `source_device_key` only 1:1 dependent/non-authoritative projection if materialized; model/ERD/transactions agree. OD-002 remains open (formal technical approval required).
- **B. Identity and licensing idempotency:** **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS (Proposed).** Scope `(tenant_id, operation_type, idempotency_key)`, canonicalization/hash, states (`in_progress`, `committed`, `failed_terminal`, `timeout_unknown_commit`), replay/conflict/concurrency/unknown-commit handling, retention/reconciliation need, and DB-local transaction coupling (A recommended default; B staged only with explicit approval) consistently documented. No exactly-once external delivery claimed. OD-008 remains open.
- **C. Event receipt deduplication:** **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS (Proposed/Open).** Explicit namespace/provenance model (`dedup_namespace_type/id` + normalized source event key); device-scoped upstream IDs must use stable source-device binding namespace to prevent cross-device collisions; data-source-global IDs may use tenant+`data_source_id`; unreliable/missing IDs require approved deterministic fallback or reject/quarantine. Distinct from identity/licensing idempotency. OD-013 remains open/partial (connector-specific behavior).
- **D. Asset lineage and relational integrity:** **PASS.** No current polymorphic `from_asset_or_op_ref` attribute/definition in model/ERD/transactions; typed `from_asset_id`/`to_asset_id` and typed nullable `merge_operation_id`/`split_operation_id` by link type documented consistently; ERD/catalogue retain no obsolete polymorphic lineage design.
- **E. Entity naming and responsibility boundaries:** **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS.** Boundaries between `role_permission`/`membership_role_assignment`, `tenant_configuration` and policy references, `ai_interaction_audit`/`audit_event`, `asset_tombstone`/terminal lifecycle state, `processing_work_item`/core processing entities, and `outbox_message`/`outbox_attempt` are consistently distinguished in intent. **Narrowly scoped essential correction applied:** removed duplicate/misplaced catalogue rows in `docs/POSTGRESQL_LOGICAL_DATA_MODEL.md` to restore authoritative catalogue consistency (9 rows removed; remaining backticked repetitions are intentional section 9.5 comparison rows). Technical design intent unchanged.
