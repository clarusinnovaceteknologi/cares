## 5. OD-001–OD-013 reconciliation summary (authoritative fidelity)
All OD-001–OD-013 were reconciled in the authoritative register and cross-referenced governance documents to explicitly and completely reflect owner-approved principles, bounded conditions/prohibitions, deferred technical details, dependencies, implementation authorization status, related increments (where applicable), and unresolved-blocker status:
- **OD-001 (ADR 0006, in principle, bounded):** atomic per-tenant monitored-device licensing/accounting (not CARES software subscription license); one unique monitored device = one licensed asset/tenant; no multi-source double-counting; equal device-type weight (no OS/vendor/device-type/telemetry weighting); licensing separate from technical quotas; PostgreSQL transactional authority; no oversubscription/duplicate business effects under concurrency; dependencies on OD-003/OD-004/OD-008 preserved; not unconditional formal ADR acceptance; ADR 0006 remains **Proposed**; not implementation authorization.
- **OD-002:** `core.asset_data_source` sole authoritative stable source-device binding owner; `source_device_key_id` immutable for lifecycle; no `core.source_device_key` in initial implementation; dependent one-to-one non-authoritative projection only if justified with same lifecycle; raw IDs/fingerprint aliases cannot replace authoritative binding; fingerprints supporting evidence only; dependencies on OD-006/OD-007/OD-013 preserved.
- **OD-003:** pending reservation principle bounded (not committed/licensed asset, not activation/committed accounting; prevent oversubscription/double-counting under concurrency); dependencies on OD-004/OD-001; mechanics/lifecycle/expiration/recovery/concurrency/accounting deferred.
- **OD-004:** accounting counters/reconciliation principles bounded (atomicity, no double-count/oversubscription, reconciliation detects/corrects drift, committed vs pending distinction); dependencies on OD-001/OD-003/OD-008; mechanisms/scheduling/repair/locking/edge cases/numeric-operational policies deferred.
- **OD-005:** merge/split principles bounded (preserved lineage/history, typed relationally enforceable lineage, explicit authorization/auditability, consistent licensing accounting consequences, no silent loss of evidence/provenance, no implicit/unauthorized merge/split); edge cases/transition rules/accounting implementation deferred.
- **OD-006:** fingerprints supporting evidence (not authoritative identity); keyed cryptographic construction; key managed outside DB; rotation must not break historical identity continuity or authoritative binding; fingerprint alone insufficient to authorize merge; raw IDs/aliases must not displace authoritative binding; algorithm/KMS/rotation/versioning/custody details deferred.
- **OD-007:** all 10 approved points preserved (distinct identities; reinstall/credential rotation do not auto-create licensed asset; re-enrollment requires continuity verification; old credentials revocable/not auto-inherited; cloned devices not auto same; no implicit authoritative-binding change; enrollment authorized/auditable with replay protection; cross-tenant transfer explicit lifecycle transition; no continuity assumption from similar identifiers/fingerprints); protocol/continuity/credential/replay/retirement mechanics deferred.
- **OD-008:** scope `(tenant_id, operation_type, idempotency_key)`; deterministic versioned canonical representation/hash; same scope/key/hash replay without duplicate business effect; same scope/key different hash terminal conflict/no second mutation; durable for retry/reconciliation horizon; durable states `in_progress`/`committed`/`failed_terminal`; `timeout_unknown_commit` is caller-side unknown-outcome condition in default single-transaction model (not automatically a durable ledger state in that model); single-transaction coupling default for DB-local ops; staged/multi-transaction only with demonstrated need and separate approval; retention aligned to retry/reconciliation horizon (duration deferred); event receipt dedup separate (OD-013); external delivery at-least-once; does not authorize I-06/I-13/implementation.
- **OD-009:** same PostgreSQL transaction for business state + outbox; leases/fencing for internal transitions; obsolete fencing token cannot update internal outbox state; lease expiry does not prove external delivery failure; explicit `delivery_unknown`; at-least-once external delivery (duplicates possible), stable logical message identity, per-attempt identity/status/audit trail; retry/replay/reconciliation separate; deferred operational/implementation details preserved; does not authorize I-09/I-13/implementation.
- **OD-010:** evidence integrity/provenance/auditability preserved; no silent overwrite/erase; retention by category/purpose/need; legal hold (scope/reason/authorization/audit trail/review) prevents automated deletion in scope; deletion checks obligations/holds/dependencies/ownership including derived data/copies under CARES control; transparent backup/external-copy limitations; idempotent/resumable/verifiable deletion; asset vs evidence lifecycle separate; tombstone not justification for indefinite raw retention; deferred governance/technical specifics preserved.
